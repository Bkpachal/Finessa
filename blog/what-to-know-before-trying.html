Before ordering any Gut & Liver Health Drink Mix (powder supplement), it helps to know exactly what you are buying, how it is meant to be used, and what happens if it does not work out. Here is a straight, honest rundown of Finessa covering each of those points.

What Finessa Actually Contains

Finessa lists 4 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • Polyphenols — Polyphenols are antioxidant-rich plant compounds included in Finessa to help protect cells from oxidative stress and support a balanced gut environment.
  • Probiotics (5 billion CFU) — Each serving supplies 5 billion colony-forming units of probiotic bacteria intended to help populate the gut with beneficial microbes and support digestive balance.
  • Dandelion — Dandelion root is a long-used botanical in liver and digestive formulas, included in Finessa to support normal bile flow and healthy digestion.
  • Milk Thistle — Milk thistle contains silymarin, a well-known plant compound associated with liver support, and is included in Finessa as a core liver ingredient.

How It Is Meant To Be Used

The label calls for 1 scoop per day, with 50 servings in each bottle. Individual ingredient amounts are listed on the label. Consistency day to day matters more than any single dose.

How It Is Meant To Work

Finessa pairs live probiotic cultures with polyphenol antioxidants and two traditional liver botanicals, aiming to support digestion and liver function from two directions at once.

Seed the gut with probiotics: Each scoop delivers 5 billion CFU of live probiotic cultures that are intended to help replenish beneficial bacteria in the digestive tract.

Feed and protect with polyphenols: Plant polyphenols act as antioxidants, helping to counter oxidative stress while supporting a favorable environment for gut microbes.

Support the liver with botanicals: Milk thistle and dandelion are included to support normal liver function and healthy bile flow, which in turn plays a role in digestion.

Build consistency with a drink: Because Finessa is a powder you mix into water, it fits easily into a morning or evening routine, and consistency is what makes daily gut and liver support meaningful.
