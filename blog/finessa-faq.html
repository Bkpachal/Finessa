What exactly is Finessa?

Finessa is a powdered drink mix formulated for gut and liver support. It combines polyphenols, 5 billion CFU of probiotics, dandelion and milk thistle in a single daily scoop.

How do I use Finessa?

Digestive and liver support supplements are typically used consistently for several weeks before people report noticeable differences. Many users treat it as a daily habit rather than a quick fix, so give it a few weeks of regular use.

Is Finessa safe? Are there side effects?

Finessa is made from probiotics and plant ingredients that are generally well tolerated. Some people may experience mild digestive adjustment in the first days of probiotic use. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication or managing a health condition, talk to your doctor before starting.
